Driver Bank:		078000 (W)
General Pointer List:	8000 (W) (Z80 Bank Offset)
Tempo List:		8027 (W) (Z80 Bank Offset)
Music Pointer List:	803B (W) (Z80 Bank Offset)
SFX Pointer List:	0F6C (W) (Z80 RAM)
Instr. Pointer List:	8063 (W) (Z80 Bank Offset)
Mod. Pointer List:	803B (W) (Z80 Bank Offset, list empty)
PSG Pointer List:	803B (W) (Z80 Bank Offset, list empty, PSG support removed)
DAC Voice Pointer List:	015E (W) (Z80 RAM)
Z80 Snd Driver Offset:	07E8CE (W)
Sound RAM:		1C00 (Z80 RAM)


DAC List Format: (all little endian values)
2 bytes - Start Offset (Z80 Bank Offset)
2 bytes - Data Length

Data is DPCM compressed with the pre-SMPS delta-array.
01 03 06 0C 18 28 50 90 FF FD FA F4 E8 D8 B0 70 (Z80 Driver offset 014E)


Note:
- This sound driver is largely on the one from Ghouls 'N Ghosts. It is a preSMPS Z80 driver modified to play the Capcom sequence format.
  Unlike Ghouls 'N Ghosts, support for SMPS sound effects was stripped off.
- Sound ID C3 causes the sound driver to play a short PCM sample. (The routines were apparently copy-pasted from Rambo III.)
  The offsets are hardcoded into the Z80 driver (code offset 00E0).
  Data Start: 015E
  Data Length: 0170
