There are a few additional tools that can decompress data from certain games. You can find them in the respective game-folder in the Rips-category.

Z80\Dyna Brothers\DynaBrothers_Extract
	Decompresses data from Dyna Brothers. (uses LZSS and LZHUF-like compressions)
Z80\Sega CD BIOS\SCDBIOS_Extract
	Extracts the 68k and Z80 sound drivers and their data from the Sega MegaCD ROMs.
Z80\Sorcerian\Sorcerian_Extract
	Decompresses the music data from Sorcerian. (LZSS compression)
